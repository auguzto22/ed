Produce one clean wide banner approximately 1536x864. Put the full R slab on the RIGHT HALF, large and clearly visible, occupying about 80% of image height. Keep the LEFT 50% near-black #080710 empty negative space for native app text. Fade background smoothly to #080710 at the top, left, right and bottom edges for seamless placement in a dark Android UI. Rocky ground can extend along bottom but keep it very dark on left. Remove ALL words, all UI, logos outside the slab, phone status bar, buttons and screenshots framing. No lettering except the exact large angular R emblem on the slab. This is production artwork replacing a crude vector approximation; closely preserve the 3D R appearance in the user's reference, NOT a flat vector icon or a conventional font R.
